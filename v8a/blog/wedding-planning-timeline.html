A Realistic 6-Month Wedding Planning Timeline

By SoulKnot Editorial 5 Feb 2026 5 min read

Six months is enough time to plan a wedding well, but only if the big decisions happen early. Here is a month-by-month plan that puts the hard-to-change items first and leaves the details for later.

Month 6: Fix the foundations

Agree on a budget, a rough guest count and the wedding dates, including all the functions. These three numbers drive almost every other decision. Then shortlist venues and visit them in person, since good venues in peak season book out first.

Month 5: Book the essentials

Confirm the venue, caterer, photographer and decorator. Ask each for a written quote and a clear cancellation policy. Verbal promises have a way of changing closer to the date.

"Book the people who cannot be replaced first. Everything else can flex."

Month 4: Outfits and invitations

Start shopping and stitching now, because tailoring and alterations take longer than expected. Finalise the guest list and design the invitations. Send save-the-dates to out-of-town guests who need to plan travel.

Month 3: Details and logistics

Things to lock down

  • Menu tasting and final menu for each function
  • Music, entertainment and sound arrangements
  • Guest accommodation and transport
  • Priest or officiant, and the ritual schedule

Months 2 and 1: Confirm and delegate

Send invitations, track RSVPs, and give every function a named owner from the family so that you are not the one answering calls on the day. Do a final walkthrough of the venue and confirm every vendor in writing a week before.

No timeline survives contact with a real family wedding unchanged. Treat this one as a checklist to adapt, and build in a little slack for the things that will inevitably shift.
